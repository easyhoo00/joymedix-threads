Data Deletion Instructions — JOYMEDIX Publisher

To have data that JOYMEDIX Publisher obtained through the Threads API deleted:

  1. Send an e-mail to joymedix@joymedix.co.kr with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your Threads username and, if applicable, the link to the post you want removed from our records.
  3. We delete the data from our systems within 14 days and reply to confirm.

If you are the owner of the connected account, you can also revoke the App's access at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → JOYMEDIX Publisher → Remove.
