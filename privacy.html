Privacy Policy — JOYMEDIX Publisher

Effective date: 27 September 2026

JOYMEDIX Publisher ("the App") is an internal tool operated by JOYMEDIX Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"). It connects to the Threads API on behalf of our own Threads account (@joymedix) to publish our posts, read replies to our posts, view our post insights, search public Threads posts for a small set of industry keywords, and post replies from our account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access through the Threads API

  • Our own account data: profile information, our posts, replies to our posts and insights for our posts.
  • Public posts returned by keyword search: post ID, author username, post text, permalink and timestamp of public Threads posts that match the keywords we search (for example product names such as "Rejuran distributor").

2. How we use it

We use our own account data to schedule and publish our posts and to measure their performance. We use public keyword-search results so that a member of our staff can decide whether to reply to a post from our account, offering information about our products to clinics and distributors who publicly ask for suppliers. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making about individuals, and we do not sell it.

3. Storage and retention

Data is stored on a company-owned computer in the Republic of Korea. For public posts we store only the post ID, author username, post text and permalink needed to post a reply, and we delete search results that we do not act on within 30 days. Access tokens are stored locally and are never published.

4. Sharing

We do not share Threads platform data with third parties. Service providers we use (for example, our hosting and e-mail providers) process data only on our instructions.

5. Your rights and data deletion

If you are a Threads user whose public post appears in our search results and you want us to delete it, or if you are the account owner and want your data removed, e-mail joymedix@joymedix.co.kr with the post link or account name. We delete the data within 14 days and confirm by e-mail. See also our data deletion instructions.
